Information for your trip

What to know before you travel to Cusco, Machu Picchu and Tambopata.

Best time to travel Altitude and soroche How to get to Machu Picchu Machu Picchu tickets How to get to Tambopata

01

Best time to travel

The dry season runs from May to September: sunny days and cold nights in Cusco. It is also the busiest season.

December to March is the rainy season. The Inca Trail closes in February for maintenance.

02

Altitude and soroche

Cusco is at about 3,400 m; Machu Picchu, lower down, at about 2,430 m. On your first day, rest, eat light and drink water.

Leave the highest tours —Rainbow Mountain, over 5,000 m, and Humantay Lake, at about 4,200 m— until you have spent two or three days in Cusco.

03

How to get to Machu Picchu

By train: from Cusco or Ollantaytambo to Aguas Calientes, then by bus to the entrance.

Overland: by car to Hidroeléctrica and about three hours on foot along the railway.

On foot: on the Inca Trail, with an official permit, or on alternative routes such as Salkantay.

04

Machu Picchu tickets

Tickets are sold by time slot and by circuit, with a limited daily quota. You need your original passport or ID.

Huayna Picchu and Machu Picchu Mountain have separate quotas, even more limited.

05

How to get to Tambopata

Puerto Maldonado has an airport with flights from Cusco and Lima. From Cusco you can also go by bus on the Interoceanic Highway.

From Puerto Maldonado, the lodges are reached by boat on the Madre de Dios and Tambopata rivers.

Frequently asked questions

Is the price per person?

Yes. All prices on this website are per person. For groups or a private tour, write to us and we will send you a quote.

How can I pay?

With Yape, Plin, bank transfer, card, PayPal or cash. Before any payment, we confirm availability and the price with you on WhatsApp.

What language does the guide speak?

Our guides speak Spanish and English. If you need another language, ask when you book and we will arrange it.

Where and when will I be picked up?

All tours from Cusco include pickup at your hotel (confirmed by the agency on 25 Sep). The exact time depends on the tour: we confirm it on WhatsApp when you book. [Pickup times for each tour: to be confirmed]

What shoes do I need?

Comfortable sneakers are enough for the cultural tours. For the mountains and treks, hiking shoes or shoes with a good grip, because there are stretches of dirt and stone. In the rainforest, shoes that can get wet.

When is the best time to visit Cusco and Machu Picchu?

The dry season, from May to September, has clearer skies. From December to March it rains more, but there are fewer people.

What is altitude sickness (soroche) and how can I avoid it?

It is the discomfort caused by the altitude: Cusco is at about 3,400 m. Rest on the first day, eat light, drink plenty of water and leave the mountains for later.

How do I get to Tambopata?

By plane from Cusco or Lima to Puerto Maldonado, or by bus from Cusco on the Interoceanic Highway. From Puerto Maldonado, the lodges are reached by boat.
