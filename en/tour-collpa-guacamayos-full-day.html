Full Day Macaw Clay Lick

  • Duration1 day
  • DifficultyEasy
  • Altitudelowland rainforest
  • GuideSpanish and English; other languages on request

Overview

A day trip from Puerto Maldonado to the Chuncho clay lick, one of the largest macaw clay licks in the world.

Places you will visit

  • Puerto Maldonado
  • Filadelfia community
  • Chuncho clay lick

Itinerary Full program

01Chuncho clay lick
  1. Departure at 3:00 a.m. from Puerto Maldonado, with pickup anywhere in the city.
  2. About 2 hours by car to the Filadelfia community.
  3. 30 minutes by boat to the Chuncho clay lick.
  4. Watching the macaws and other Amazon birds come down in the early morning to eat minerals.
  5. Return to the city by the same route.
