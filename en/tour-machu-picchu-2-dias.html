Machu Picchu 2 Days / 1 Night

  • Duration2 days / 1 night
  • DifficultyEasy
  • Altitude2,430 m
  • GuideSpanish and English; other languages on request

Overview

The Sacred Valley on the connecting tour, the train to Machu Picchu Pueblo, a night there and a guided visit to the citadel the next day.

Places you will visit

  • Cusco
  • Sacred Valley
  • Ollantaytambo
  • Machu Picchu Pueblo
  • Machu Picchu

Itinerary

01Day 1 · Sacred Valley and train to Machu Picchu
  1. Connecting tour through the Sacred Valley.
  2. Train to Machu Picchu Pueblo.
  3. Night in Machu Picchu Pueblo.
02Day 2 · Machu Picchu and return
  1. Bus up to Machu Picchu.
  2. Guided visit.
  3. Bus down.
  4. Train back to Ollantaytambo and bus to Cusco.
